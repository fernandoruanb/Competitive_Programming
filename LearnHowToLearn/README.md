> Hello everyone.
>
> I decided to remove the material for now. Even though my intention was good, I realized that I need to focus first on my own transformation, mature my ideas more, and, in the future, create something truly original to help others with more responsibility.
>
> Still, I hope at least one message remained clear: **learning exists, it is real, and we can learn how to learn.**
>
> Your mistakes do not define who you are. Difficulties, delays, failures, and external judgments are not the final sentence of your life. There is an internal learning system that can be developed. We can become stronger, more conscious, more capable, and freer people.
>
> Keep going. Fight. Believe in your own potential and in your own progress. Maybe the path is difficult, maybe it takes time, but that does not mean you are broken. It means you are still under construction.
>
> I also highly recommend iCanStudy and Dr. Justin Sung’s work. For me, it was an extremely important reference that helped me see that real change is possible, and that learning better can transform a life.
>
> Do not let other people define who you are.
> Transcend your limits.
> Develop your inner apprentices.
>
> Everything will be okay.
